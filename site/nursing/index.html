Questions nurses ask

Does Domi replace our clinical system?

It replaces the admin around the visits: the diary, the spreadsheets, the referral and cancellation forms, and the ringing round when someone cancels. Care plans, results and prescribing stay in your clinical system, and Domi works alongside it.

Can it keep fasting blood tests in the morning?

Yes. Mark a patient mornings only, or not before a time, and booking, moving a visit and route planning all keep to it.

Can we book a wound dressing twice a week for a month?

Not in one step yet; that's being built next. Today each visit is booked on its own, and Domi suggests the slots that add the least driving.

Where is patient data kept?

In London, encrypted, with daily backups we test, under a data processing agreement signed before any real patient goes in. The live demo holds invented patients only. Our security page sets out who handles the data and what each sees.
