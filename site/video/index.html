See Domi in two minutes

With music. Sample patients, real software, filmed in the demo practices for each kind of service.

  • Each kind of visit goes only to the clinicians who can do it.
  • Rounds are planned to drive the least, and patients whose time moves are texted for you.
  • Clinicians carry the day on their phone, even without signal, and the office sees at once if a visit couldn't be done.
