Questions podiatrists ask

Does Domi replace our practice system?

It replaces the admin around the visits: the diary, the spreadsheets, the referral and cancellation forms, and the ringing round when someone cancels. Foot assessments and wound records stay in your clinical system, and Domi works alongside it.

Can it book patients we see every six to eight weeks?

Not as one repeating booking yet; that's being built next. Today patients due back join the waiting list at your interval, and each visit is booked in the slot that adds the least driving.

How do patients cancel?

They reply CANCEL to the reminder text, or a relative uses the cancel link in it. No forms and no phone call, and the slot is offered straight away to someone who's waiting.

Where is patient data kept?

In London, encrypted, with daily backups we test, under a data processing agreement signed before any real patient goes in. The live demo holds invented patients only. Our security page lists every company that handles the data.
