Made for how domiciliary eye care works

  • The PCSE notice, checked as you bookDomi tracks the pre-visit notice for every NHS sight test, won't book one that can't be notified in time (48 hours, or 3 weeks for three or more patients at one address), and lists the notifications to lodge or cancel when visits move.
  • Tests go to optometrists onlySight tests and eye examinations are only booked with an optometrist. Dispensing opticians get the deliveries, fittings and repairs.
  • Referrals with a real reasonFamilies, carers and care homes refer through your own form. It asks for the condition that keeps the patient at home, because “housebound” alone won't do on a claim.
  • Short visits fill short gapsA gap too small for a sight test is offered for a glasses delivery or a repair instead, so the time still earns.

Questions opticians ask

Does Domi replace our practice system?

It replaces the admin around the visits: the diary, the spreadsheets, the referral and cancellation forms, and the ringing round when someone cancels. Today the eye examination stays in the clinical system you already use, and Domi works alongside it. Bringing the examination and GOS claims into Domi, so one system does both, is planned once PCSE approves Domi as a connected system.

Does it handle NHS domiciliary sight tests?

Yes. Domi checks the PCSE notice as you book: 48 hours for one or two patients at an address, 3 weeks for three or more, and never more than 8 weeks ahead, unless you record that the ICB has approved it. It keeps a list of notifications to lodge, move or cancel, with the form details ready to copy into PCSE Online.

What if the optometrist has no signal?

The day's round and records are saved on the phone, so it keeps working and catches up when signal returns. Signing out wipes them from the phone.

How do patients cancel?

They reply CANCEL to the reminder text, or a relative uses the cancel link in it. No forms and no phone call, and the slot is offered straight away to someone who's waiting.

Where is patient data kept?

In London, encrypted, with daily backups we test, under a data processing agreement signed before any real patient goes in. The live demo holds invented patients only. Our security page sets out who handles the data and what each sees.
