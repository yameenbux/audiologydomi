Questions dentists ask

Does Domi replace our dental software?

It replaces the admin around the visits: the diary, the spreadsheets, the referral and cancellation forms, and the ringing round when someone cancels. Charting, treatment plans, X-rays and NHS claims stay in your dental software, and Domi works alongside it.

Can it handle care-home sessions?

Yes. Each resident has their own record and visit, with the home's address, access and the carer to speak to, and the visits sit together on the same round. Booking a whole session with the home as one visit is being built next.

How do patients cancel?

They reply CANCEL to the reminder text, or a relative uses the cancel link in it. No forms and no phone call, and the slot is offered straight away to someone who's waiting.

Where is patient data kept?

In London, encrypted, with daily backups we test, under a data processing agreement signed before any real patient goes in. The live demo holds invented patients only. Our security page sets out who handles the data and what each sees.
