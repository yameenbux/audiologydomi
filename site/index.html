Cancellations that fill themselves.

Domi is the diary for domiciliary opticians. When a patient cancels, it texts the best-matched person on your waiting list, books the first yes, and updates the clinician’s phone.

Laptop, tablet and phone · Nothing to install · Built for home visits

The demo uses invented patients. If nobody has opened it for a while, it takes about a minute to wake.

  • Refills on its own

    A cancelled visit is offered to the best-matched patient by text. The first yes is booked, and everyone else keeps their place.

  • Books by the road

    New visits go where they add the least driving, weighed against how soon the patient needs to be seen.

  • On every clinician’s phone

    Today’s round, records and prescriptions. Changes arrive in a second, and it keeps working without signal.

Questions

Questions people ask.

Do we have to replace our current system?

No. You can run Domi for the diary, refills and routes and keep your clinical records where they are. Moving everything across is on the roadmap, and we’ll do the import with you when it’s ready.

How does it handle PCSE notifications?

An NHS sight test at home needs a notification to PCSE at least 48 hours before the visit, so Domi never offers one inside that. A gap today goes to someone waiting for a glasses delivery, a repair or a private visit instead. When an NHS test is booked or cancelled, Domi adds it to a list of notifications to lodge or cancel, with the deadline. You still lodge them in PCSE Online yourself: sending them from Domi needs PCSE to accept Domi as a connected system, and we won’t give you a date we can’t keep.

What does it cost?

We’re working with a small number of founding practices and agree pricing with each one, based on how many clinicians you have. Email us and we’ll give you a straight figure.

Does it work on a laptop, a tablet and a phone?

Yes. Domi runs in the web browser, so there’s nothing to install. The office screen fits a laptop or a tablet, and the clinician app works on any phone, or on a tablet if a clinician prefers a bigger screen.

Do patients need to download anything?

No. Patients deal with text messages and, if they want to book themselves, a simple web page with large text and one question at a time.

What if nobody replies to an offer?

Each offer has a reply window that fits how soon the visit is. When it closes, the next people are asked automatically. If there’s no longer time for anyone to get ready, the slot is left open rather than offered to someone who can’t make it.

What happens if a clinician loses their phone?

The office can see every signed-in device and sign the lost phone out remotely. Signing out wipes the round and records the phone was keeping for working offline.

Is the demo real?

The software is. The patients, clinicians and texts are invented, and the demo resets itself. Every visitor gets their own private copy, so you can cancel, book and break things freely.
