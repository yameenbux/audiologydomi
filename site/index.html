How a cancellation refills

A gap in the diary, filled before you've put the kettle on.

Every home-visit practice knows the cost of a cancelled morning: a clinician on the road with nobody to see, and a waiting list of people who'd have taken the slot.

  1. A visit is cancelled

    The office cancels it, or the patient replies CANCEL to their day-before reminder. Either way, the refill starts at once.

  2. Domi ranks the waiting list

    Clinical priority first, then whether they live in the clinician's patch, how long they've waited, and who they prefer to see. It only considers people who are home at that time and whose visit fits the gap, including the drive.

  3. Offers go out by text

    With days to spare, it asks one person at a time. With under an hour, it asks the top three at once and the first YES gets it. Too close to confirm anyone, and the slot is left open rather than wasted.

  4. The clinician's phone updates

    Within a second: the new patient, their address and their notes. Everyone else who was asked is told the slot has gone, and keeps their place.

Built for the road

Books by the drive, not just the clock.

A free slot across the city isn't really free. Domi searches every clinician's round for the week and ranks each gap by the extra driving it adds.

  • Road driving times between full postcodes, for the drive in and the drive on to the next patient.
  • Urgency counts too. Urgent patients are pulled towards sooner days; routine ones towards less driving.
  • Every day on a map, with each leg's drive time and the drive home.

On the clinician's phone

Their whole day, even without signal.

No app to install: it opens in the phone's browser. Today's round, each patient's record and audiogram, and one tap for "On my way", which texts the patient.

  • Works in a dead spot. Today's round and records are kept on the phone; notes and updates made offline sync when signal returns.
  • Alerts where they matter: care-home sign-in, who will be there, how the patient prefers to be contacted.
  • Signing out wipes the copy. A lost phone can be signed out from the office.

Everything else, included

The everyday jobs, done properly.

Day-before reminders

Every visit tomorrow gets a text at the time you choose. A CANCEL reply refills the slot a day ahead.

Online booking for patients

Large text, one question at a time. It only offers times when a clinician is already nearby.

Recalls that fill gaps

Patients due a review join the refill pool, so a cancellation near them goes to someone due anyway.

Mileage and utilisation

Booked time, driving time and estimated miles per clinician, valued at HMRC's 45p rate. Downloads as CSV.

Invoicing

Completing a visit raises the invoice at your price for that visit type. Mark it paid, void it or print it.

Two-factor sign-in

Password plus a code from an authenticator app. Accounts lock after repeated wrong passwords.

A full audit trail

Every booking, offer, text and change is logged with who did it and when.

Live, everywhere

A change in the office reaches the clinician's phone in about a second, and back again.

Before you ask

Questions people ask.

Do we have to replace our current system?

No. You can run Domi for the diary, refills and routes and keep your clinical records where they are. Moving everything across is on the roadmap, and we'll do the import with you when it's ready.

Does it work with Noah?

Not yet. Noah integration needs a partnership with HIMSA, which is on our roadmap but outside our control, so we won't give you a date we can't keep.

Do patients need to download anything?

No. Patients deal with text messages and, if they want to book themselves, a simple web page. Clinicians use the app in their phone's browser.

What if nobody replies to an offer?

Each offer has a reply window that fits how soon the visit is. When it closes, the next people are asked automatically. If there's no longer time for anyone to get ready, the slot is left open rather than offered to someone who can't make it.

What does it cost?

We're working with a small number of founding practices and agree pricing with each one, based on how many clinicians you have. Email us and we'll give you a straight figure.

Is the demo real?

The software is. The patients, clinicians and texts are invented, and the demo resets itself. Every visitor gets their own private copy, so you can cancel, book and break things freely.
