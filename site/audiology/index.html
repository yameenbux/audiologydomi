Made for hearing care at home

Visiting people with hearing loss has its own needs, starting with how you reach them.

  • Texts first, for patients who can't hear the phoneDomi contacts patients by text. The few who can only be reached by a call are flagged, with the number to ring, so nobody is missed.
  • The right clinician for each visitHearing assessments go to audiologists or hearing aid dispensers, and wax removal only to those trained for it. You tell us who does what.
  • Reviews come round on timePatients due an aid review or reassessment join the waiting list automatically, so they're first in line for a cancelled slot nearby.
  • Referrals from families and care homesRelatives, carers and care-home staff use your own referral form, and it lands straight in the office queue.

Questions audiologists ask

Does Domi replace our practice system?

It replaces the admin around the visits: the diary, the spreadsheets, the referral and cancellation forms, and the ringing round when someone cancels. Hearing tests and audiograms stay in your audiology software, such as Noah, and Domi works alongside it.

Can it handle care-home visits?

Yes. Each resident has their own record and visit, with the home's address, access and carer notes, and the visits sit on the same round.

How do patients cancel?

They reply CANCEL to the reminder text, or a relative uses the cancel link in it. No forms and no phone call, and the slot is offered straight away to someone who's waiting.

Where is patient data kept?

In London, encrypted, with daily backups we test, under a data processing agreement signed before any real patient goes in. The live demo holds invented patients only. Our security page lists every company that handles the data.
