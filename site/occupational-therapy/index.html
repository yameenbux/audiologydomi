Questions OTs ask

Does Domi replace our clinical system?

It replaces the admin around the visits: the diary, the spreadsheets, the referral and cancellation forms, and the ringing round when someone cancels. Assessment reports, care plans and equipment orders stay in your clinical system, and Domi works alongside it.

Can a long assessment and a quick equipment check sit on the same day?

Yes. Each visit type has its own length, so a 90-minute assessment and a 30-minute equipment review sit on one round, with the driving between them planned.

Can it take referrals from social workers and discharge teams?

Yes. They use the same referral form as families and GPs. It asks what's needed and why, and lands in the office queue to accept in one click.

Where is patient data kept?

In London, encrypted, with daily backups we test, under a data processing agreement signed before any real patient goes in. The live demo holds invented patients only. Our security page sets out who handles the data and what each sees.
