Cookies

This website sets no cookies. Our visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) are cookieless: they don’t store anything on your device or follow you between sites, which is why there’s no cookie banner.

The live demo sets one cookie, domi_session, when you sign in. It keeps you signed in, is strictly necessary for the demo to work, and ends when you sign out or after 12 hours at most. The demo also remembers your demo code and chosen practice in your browser’s storage, so a page reload doesn’t start a new demo. On a clinician’s phone it also keeps that day’s round, so the app works without signal; signing out wipes it. Strictly necessary storage like this doesn’t need consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations.

Who processes it for us

  • GitHub hosts this website (GitHub’s privacy statement).
  • Cloudflare provides the anonymous visit statistics (Cloudflare’s privacy policy).
  • Web3Forms delivers contact-form messages to our inbox, and keeps a copy for up to 3 years (Web3Forms’ privacy policy). If you’d rather it didn’t, email us instead of using the form.
  • Render hosts the live demo (Render’s privacy policy).
  • Our email provider holds the messages we exchange with you.

Some of these companies are based in, or process data in, the United States. Where your information leaves the UK, it’s protected by the safeguards the UK GDPR requires, such as the UK–US data bridge or standard contractual clauses. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.
